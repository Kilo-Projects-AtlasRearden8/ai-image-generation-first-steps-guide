# Writing prompts

A prompt is the description you give the AI so it knows what to draw. Think of it like telling a friend what to sketch: the clearer you are, the closer the picture gets to what you have in mind.

You do not need special keywords, magic phrases, or art experience. Plain English works fine.

## The simple prompt formula

Start with this pattern:

**Subject + Action + Setting + Style**

| Part | What it means | Example |
|------|---------------|---------|
| Subject | Who or what is in the picture | a golden retriever puppy |
| Action | What is happening | wearing a tiny raincoat |
| Setting | Where it is | sitting on a city sidewalk |
| Style | How it looks | watercolor painting, soft colors |

Put it together: "a golden retriever puppy wearing a tiny raincoat, sitting on a city sidewalk, watercolor painting, soft colors."

## Examples to copy and adapt

**Photograph:** "A close-up photograph of a steaming cup of coffee on a wooden table, morning light, soft focus background."

**Fantasy art:** "A floating island with a waterfall falling into clouds, bright colors, digital painting, fantasy book cover style."

**Cartoon:** "A friendly robot baking cookies in a kitchen, cartoon style, thick outlines, cheerful colors."

**Simple:** "A red apple on a white background." (Great for testing a new model.)

## Tips that actually help

- **Be specific about the subject.** "A dog" is fine, but "a scruffy terrier with one floppy ear" gives the AI something concrete to draw.
- **Mention lighting if it matters.** "Golden hour," "neon lights," "overcast sky," and "candlelight" all change the mood.
- **Mention the camera angle if it matters.** "Close-up," "wide shot," "bird's-eye view," and "macro" are useful words.
- **Say what you do NOT want.** Some tools have a "negative prompt" box. If you keep getting blurry images, type "blurry" there.
- **One idea at a time.** A prompt like "a cat and a dog and a dragon and a castle in space under the sea" usually turns into a mess. Pick one scene.

## Common beginner mistakes

- **Writing a novel.** Long, rambling prompts often confuse the model. Start short, then add details.
- **Expecting exact text.** AI image models are getting better at words, but they still misspell things. Do not rely on the image for perfect signs or logos.
- **Asking for real people by name.** Many free tools block prompts that name celebrities or public figures. Use descriptions instead: "a woman in a 1920s dress" rather than a specific actor's name.

## When the image is not what you wanted

That is normal. AI images take a few tries. Change one word at a time and generate again. If the puppy looks too big, add "small." If the colors are too dull, add "vibrant." Small tweaks teach you how the model thinks.
