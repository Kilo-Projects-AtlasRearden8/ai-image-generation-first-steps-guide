# Prompt checklist

Print this page or copy it into your notes. Work through it every time you make an AI image. It takes less than a minute and saves you from do-overs.

## Before you generate

- [ ] I know what I want to show (subject, action, setting).
- [ ] I picked an aspect ratio (1:1, 16:9, 9:16, etc.).
- [ ] I chose a tool (cloud or local).
- [ ] I checked the tool's daily free limit, if any.
- [ ] My prompt does not name a real person or a specific living artist.
- [ ] I am not pasting secrets, passwords, or personal data into the prompt.

## Writing the prompt

- [ ] My prompt follows Subject + Action + Setting + Style.
- [ ] I mentioned lighting if it matters (morning light, neon, overcast, etc.).
- [ ] I mentioned camera angle if it matters (close-up, wide shot, etc.).
- [ ] I kept it to one scene, not a dozen ideas at once.
- [ ] I added a style word (photograph, watercolor, digital painting, cartoon).

## After you generate

- [ ] I looked at the image and decided what to change, if anything.
- [ ] I changed only one word at a time when retrying.
- [ ] I saved the image and the prompt that made it, so I can recreate it.
- [ ] I checked the tool's terms if I plan to share or sell the image.
- [ ] I did not claim the image is a real photograph of an event.

## Master checklist: ready to share

- [ ] The image matches my idea well enough.
- [ ] The aspect ratio fits where I will use it.
- [ ] I know whether I likely own copyright (U.S.: AI-only images generally are not copyrightable).
- [ ] I am comfortable sharing it publicly.
- [ ] I did not use someone's name or likeness without permission.

Keep a printed copy by your computer. Check off each box as you go.
