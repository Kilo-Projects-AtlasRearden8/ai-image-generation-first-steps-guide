# Choosing models

An AI image model is the engine that turns your words into a picture. Different models are good at different things: some look like photographs, some look like paintings, some are great at faces, and some are better at fantasy scenes.

The good news: you can start with excellent free models. You do not need a credit card, and you do not need to buy a subscription.

## Where to make images for free

Here are the most beginner-friendly free options:

| Option | Cost | What it is | Best for |
|--------|------|------------|----------|
| Bing Image Creator (Microsoft Designer) | Free | Web-based, uses DALL-E 3 | Your very first images; simple prompts |
| Leonardo.ai | Free daily credits | Web-based, several models | Trying different styles |
| SeaArt.ai | Free daily credits | Web-based, many community models | Exploring lots of models |
| Civitai | Free browsing; some generation credits | Community model library | Finding niche art styles |
| Stable Diffusion (local) | Free | Software you install on your own computer | Privacy; no internet needed after install |
| ComfyUI (local) | Free | Node-based interface for Stable Diffusion | Power users who like to tweak everything |
| Fooocus (local) | Free | Simple local interface for Stable Diffusion | Local images with simple controls |

Start with Bing Image Creator or Leonardo.ai. They work in your browser, require no installation, and give you enough free generations to learn with.

## What to look for in a model

When you pick a model, ask yourself three questions:

1. **What am I making?** A realistic photo needs a different model than a cartoon or an oil painting.
2. **Do I want privacy?** Cloud models (Bing, Leonardo) send your prompt to their servers. Local models (Stable Diffusion, Fooocus) run on your computer, so nothing leaves your machine.
3. **How much control do I want?** Web tools are simple but limited. Local tools let you change almost everything, but they take longer to learn.

## Free model families you will see

- **DALL-E 3** — Great at following detailed prompts. Good for beginners because it "understands" what you ask for.
- **Stable Diffusion (SD 1.5, SDXL, SD 3.5)** — The most popular open models. Huge community, tons of free add-ons, works locally.
- **Flux** — Newer open model with excellent text-in-image quality and realistic lighting.
- **Midjourney** — Beautiful results, but usually a paid subscription. Not covered here because this manual focuses on free tools.

## My suggestion for your first week

1. Open Bing Image Creator and make five images with simple prompts.
2. Try Leonardo.ai or SeaArt to see how a different model handles the same prompt.
3. If you want privacy or unlimited generations, install Fooocus or Stable Diffusion on your computer.

No choice is permanent. You can switch models whenever you like.
